# Current status — Crimson 4.0.0 preview

Independent Qbox implementation. See repository XR_PARITY_REVIEW_4.0.md and INSTALL/UPGRADE_4_AR.md. The earlier 3.2.5 comparison is historical.

Implemented additions: citizen mugshot/phone/wanted/append-only notes; native Qbox license grant/revoke; structured offense selection and conviction history; registered vehicle profiles; structured case participants, vehicles and image evidence; employee badge numbers/notes, accumulated duty time and Crimson suspension; discovered native businesses with owner/logo/status/employee files; safe rich-text documents, designated physical-copy signatures and inventory archive deposit/retrieval; shared armory stock/cart/personal kits/returns; case-linked tracking bands with inventory tool removal and scoped blips; responding units, gunshot alerts and an optional verified Origen alert/license-cache bridge; scoped statistics; self-consented screencapture live video with external SFU setup. The supplied readable penal-code configuration contributes 309 unique offenses.

Preserved: exact DOJ grade grants with requested Management gate; Apex bank/phone bridge; physical ox_target printer and pending-only list; chief-issued DOJ ID item with e idcard and Backspace; Units & GPS in Overview, Vehicle search in Operations, Businesses in Administration; DOJ/police/sheriff map visibility; private recorded video, fixed cameras and audited legal/payment workflows.

Limits: no claim of exact XR parity. Rich text supports headings/bold/lists, not arbitrary HTML or PDF layout. Image snapshots retain validated URLs, not third-party bytes. Statistics/duty/suspension/wanted annotations describe Crimson data, not all external resources. Convictions do not automatically debit/jail. Armory restocking is an authorized administrative action, not economic procurement. Origen bridge is one-way alert import and native-license cache invalidation; no shared Origen assignment/evidence/bodycam database. Live video needs a deployed screencapture SFU Worker; game audio is unavailable. No bundled helicopter spawning, complete locale set, automatic inventory-weapon registry or automatic debt collection. Existing configured camera coverage remains map-specific. Business MDT access stays disabled as requested.

Validation: automated Lua/DOM/provider mocks and real MariaDB schema/concurrency checks. Actual FiveM providers/natives and multiplayer acceptance require testing on the user's server. Interrupted inventory operations remain journaled for console reconciliation instead of automatic uncertain replays.
